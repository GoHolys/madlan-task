# Madlan — R&D Operations Engineer Challenge

Attached is a CSV of Israeli residential property deals.

**Build something useful with it and an LLM. Deploy it. Send us the URL.**

What "useful" means is your call. Deciding that is part of what we're evaluating.

This role sits between R&D, our Customer Success team and our customers. So we care about how well you
build, and just as much about how well what you build holds up in front of people who aren't engineers.

---

## Constraints

These are non-negotiable. Everything else is open.

1. **Live and public.** A URL we can open. Not localhost, not a video.
2. **Hebrew, RTL.** It's our product's native condition.
3. **The LLM does real work.** Not a wrapper around a summary.
4. **It only says what the data supports, and the user can tell what an answer is based on.** When the
   data is missing or questionable, the app says so in plain language.
5. **It fails gracefully.** When the model is slow, wrong or down, the user gets a clear and honest
   message, not a spinner that never stops or a stack trace.
6. **It's tested and debuggable.** Tests for the parts that matter most. When something breaks, you
   can tell what broke and why without guessing.
7. **A CSM could use it without you there.** Include a one-page guide written for a Customer Success
   Manager, in plain language: what the app does, what it can't do, and a reply they can send when a
   customer says "this number is wrong."

---

## How you work

Build it with AI assistance. We do. Keep a short log of how you worked, including at least one time it
gave you a bad answer and you caught it.

---

## Time

**~5 focused hours of work, 3 days to deliver.** If you finish earlier, send it earlier.

We'd rather see one thing done with real thought than four things done fast. If you cut something, tell
us what and why.

**Deliver:** the URL, a link to the repo, the CSM guide, and your AI log.

---

## About the data, and the gaps

The data is real-world shaped. We haven't cleaned it, and we're not going to tell you what's wrong
with it.

We've also left parts of this brief unspecified on purpose. Some of what you need isn't in the CSV at
all.

Decide and document your assumptions. If something truly blocks you, you can email us, but a clear,
documented decision is just as good an answer.

---

## The session — 45 minutes, in English

**Demo — 10 min.** Show it working. Tell us what you built and why.

**Code walkthrough — 15 min.** Walk us through the part of the code you're proudest of and the part
you're least happy with. Show us your tests. We'll ask you to show what happens when the model fails.
Be ready to defend the decisions your AI assistant made for you.

**The customer moment — 10 min.** We'll play a CSM who just got an upset message from a customer about
something your app showed. Work through it with us live: find out what happened, decide what to tell
them, and say it clearly.

**Q&A — 10 min.**

---

## What we're looking for

Product instinct and solid engineering you can defend. Honesty about what the data can and can't tell
you. Things that fail well. Clear communication with people who aren't engineers.

Your work stays yours. This is for evaluation only.
